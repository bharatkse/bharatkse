## 🧠 Juris-AI

**Juris-AI** is an open-source AI assistant exploring practical and
production-oriented AI workflows for legal research.

The project focuses on building reliable AI systems where retrieval,
reasoning, tools, execution, authorization, and human approval work
together.

### What I'm exploring with Juris-AI

- Legal research with AI
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- LLM-based applications
- Agentic AI workflows
- Tool-based AI execution
- Human-in-the-loop workflows
- Secure and controlled AI actions
- Production-oriented AI architecture
